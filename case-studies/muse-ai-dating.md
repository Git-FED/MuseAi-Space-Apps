# Muse AI Dating

The Muse AI Dating app is recorded as the first end-to-end Space-to-app proof that worked. It demonstrated the practical loop:

1. Build or edit the Space.
2. Share it.
3. Put the share URL into the wrapper.
4. Re-share after edits.
5. Reload the wrapper and verify the updated experience.

The key lesson is operational: the URL can stay stable while the Space content changes, but the wrapper must be reloaded after the re-share.
